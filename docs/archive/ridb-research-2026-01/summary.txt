## 💡 **Key Decisions Explained**

**Q: Why not use RIDB API?**
A: It simply doesn't contain USFS trail geometry. RIDB is focused on recreation.gov managed facilities (campgrounds, picnic areas). Trail data is maintained separately by USFS.

**Q: Why bulk download instead of waiting for an API?**
A: USFS doesn't offer a real-time API for trails—only quarterly bulk exports. Your 100-500 validation requests/day is perfectly handled by Cloud SQL with spatial indexes (<50ms queries).

**Q: Is quarterly refresh acceptable?**
A: Yes. Trail data changes infrequently. A quarterly sync (when USFS updates) is actually ideal for your use case.

**Q: What about spatial snapping accuracy?**
A: Using PostGIS `ST_ClosestPoint()`, you can snap citizen GPS points directly onto official trails. 50-meter default tolerance accounts for typical GPS noise while ensuring accuracy.
