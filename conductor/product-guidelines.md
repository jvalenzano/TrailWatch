# Product Guidelines

## Tone and Voice

The primary tone and voice for the project's brand messaging is **Professional and Authoritative**. This approach is intended to establish trust and credibility with USFS personnel and other official partners. All communications should be clear, concise, and factual.

## Visual Identity

The visual style is **Clean and Functional**. The design of the user interface and any marketing materials should prioritize usability, accessibility, and the clear presentation of data. The aesthetic should be straightforward and professional, avoiding unnecessary ornamentation.
